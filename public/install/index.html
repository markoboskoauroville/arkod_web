ARKOD Layer

Katastarske čestice Hrvatske, on your home screen

Installed, it opens full screen like any app, keeps the map and sheets you have seen for no signal, and needs no store.

iPhone and iPad your phone

  1. Open arkod-layer.pages.dev in Safari (in Chrome on iPhone the steps are the same, from its own Share button).
  2. Tap the Share button: the square with the arrow up, at the bottom of the screen (at the top on an iPad).
  3. Scroll the list down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. ARKOD Layer is now on your home screen; open it from there.

Data an installed web app keeps on an iPhone (Moje čestice, caches, the Google key) stays in that app, apart from Safari. Export Moje čestice as a file before you delete the app.

Android your phone

The Android app is the fullest ARKOD: the same map, plus the phone's own offline map of Croatia.

Download the Android app (APK)

Open the downloaded file; Android asks once to allow installs from your browser.

Or this web app: in Chrome, open the ⋮ menu → Install app (on some phones: Add to Home screen → Install).

Computer

Chrome or Edge: the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app. Safari on a Mac: File → Add to Dock.
