ARKOD Layer: how it works and how to use it

For the Android app and the web app (arkod-layer.pages.dev). The two work the same way; where they differ, it says Android or web. Settings are in English; everything of the cadastre is in Croatian, as the state writes it.

1. What the app is

ARKOD Layer puts the Croatian state's cadastral parcels (čestice) on a map, anywhere in Croatia. A tap on a parcel tells you its number; a second tap opens its sheets: the posjedovni list (who the cadastre says holds it, the land uses, the area) and the vlasnički list from the land registry (who owns it, the shares, the burdens). You can keep your own parcels, colour them, share them as files, read a whole village into the phone for use without signal, and let the app search the map for a name.

Everything comes from the State Geodetic Administration (DGU), the same public services the portal uredjenazemlja.hr uses. Nothing you do is sent anywhere else. Everything you keep stays on your device.

3. A parcel: tap, sheet, tabs

  1. Zoom to z15 or closer. Tap a parcel: its number appears, and a cyan outline is drawn round it. This works even with the ARKOD layer hidden.
  2. Tap it again: its sheet opens over the whole screen.

The sheet has three tabs:

  • uporaba: land uses (maslinik, pašnjak, kuća…) and their areas.
  • posjedovni: the possession sheet(s): holders, their shares and addresses.
  • vlasnički: the land registry folio (z.k. uložak): owners, shares, entries, burdens (tereti). The parcel numbers listed there are links: a tap goes to that parcel on the map. If the cadastre does not link a folio, the app asks for the land-book parcel number or the folio number once, and remembers it.

On top: the filter (a name, an address, anything; each tab shows how many rows fit), and CPY (copies the whole sheet as text), TXT (sends it as a text file), FILE (sends this parcel as an .arkod.json file that the app opens on another phone). At the bottom, Dodaj u Moje čestice keeps the parcel (section 5). A sheet read from the device, with no signal, says when it was read.

5. Moje čestice (my parcels)

  • On a sheet, switch on Dodaj u Moje čestice; then pick its colour and line (puna, isprekidana, točkasta). It is drawn on every map, in that colour.
  • Settings → Moje čestice: the list, in groups. Tap a group's name for its colour, line, thickness, and to show or hide it. Each parcel can be named (the pencil) or removed (the bin, twice).
  • Export a group (the share icon): an .arkod.json file with its parcels, outlines and look. Import: "Import a file" (or open the file with the app Android, drop it on the map web on a computer). It becomes a group named after the file. The same file works in both apps.

6. Parcel view and lines

A long press on ▦ opens Parcel view:

  • ARKOD layer: the state's lines on or off. Only Moje čestice: hide everything else. Parcel search: the parcel field on or off.
  • Lines: the colour of the state's lines (auto or one of six), their transparency (20 to 100 %) and thickness (Fine, Normal, Bold). The app redraws the state's picture itself, on the device.
  • Parcel caches, Moje čestice and Imenik are reached from here too.

7. Parcel caches: a whole place for no signal

Parcel view → Cache this view, give it a name. The app reads every parcel in the view: first their outlines (the WFS, 500 at a time), then every posjedovni list, then, if "Also vlasnički listovi" is on, every owner sheet once. A line says each stage, how many of how many, how fast, how long is left, and what failed; stop keeps what was read. When the state's database fails (ORA-01000), the app waits and tries again, eight times over about five minutes, and says so.

Inside a cache, its own lines are drawn (in its colour, line and thickness, with the numbers), a tap selects from the device, and the parcel field and Imenik find every name, number, sheet, address and land use in it, with no signal. Settings → Parcel caches lists them.

8. Imenik

Every holder and owner of every sheet the device has read goes into Imenik. Search it by name (Parcel view → Imenik, or simply type the name into the parcel field): each name shows its parcel, and a tap opens it.

9. The cache key, keywords, and what is kept

Everything the app reads is kept on the device: map tiles of the ARKOD layer, every sheet and owner sheet, every search, every parcel outline. Kept things are used whenever a service does not answer, so what you saw once opens again on a hillside.

The cache key ((•)) adds reading ahead: while it is lit,

  • the ARKOD tiles round where the map rests (and round you) are fetched;
  • when the map rests at z16 or closer, the parcels on the screen are found (16 points) and their sheets read;
  • when you open a sheet, the other parcels of its owner sheet are read.

A line says "cache: reading · 40 read · 12 kept". Switch it off when you do not want it (for example on a costly connection): what you open yourself is still kept.

Cache criteria (keywords), in Settings at the top: write surnames, names or anything, separated by commas, e.g. boško, gobić, maslinik. Then a sheet read in the background is kept only when a name, the place or a land use on it fits one keyword; the others are thrown away. Empty: everything is kept. Each keyword's words must start words on the sheet: bosk finds BOŠKO, boško ivana finds Ivana Boško, ana does not find IVANA.

Kept on this phone, at the top of the settings: the size, how many sheets and outlines, clear (tap twice), and Kept čestice: every parcel whose sheet is kept, by number with three words (surname · place · k.o.), with a filter. A tap opens it from the device.

10. Fly-through scanning

  1. Tap the airplane. Write what to look for: a name (jaša), several (jaša, boško ivana), a place or a land use.
  2. Move over the map at z16 or closer, and let it rest. Each time it rests, the app asks for the parcels under 25 points across the screen, reads each new one's posjedovni list (and its vlasnički list if the first says nothing).
  3. Every parcel that mentions your words gets a thick magenta outline and is selected; the line says found 2449/2 · selecting and the note says what fitted (JAŠA ANICA). Tap a found parcel for its sheet.
  4. Move on; each rest scans the new view. Tap the airplane again to land.

The line says every step: scanning 14/25 · 9 sheets read · found 1, this view scanned · move on, or zoom to 16 or closer. Everything read is kept, so flying over the same place again asks nothing. The words match the same way as keywords.

Every request the app makes sets its light. Every minute, a service not heard from for three minutes is asked one small question (about parcel 1358/3 in Kukljica). Google is never asked on its own, because every request to Google is on your key.

When a service goes down or comes back, the map says so at once, e.g. 16:31 WFS offline · ORA-01000: maximum open cursors exceeded, and it is kept in Settings → Services → Service log with the time. After a few days the log shows at which hours each service fails. Check now at the end asks them all.

Next to a service that is down (red, or grey: not asked yet) there is its own Check now button. It asks that service again, up to three times (at once, after 5 s, after 10 s), the button saying each try; the light turns green the moment it answers, or the row says "still offline" with the state's reason. When a service comes back, by this button or by itself, what was waiting for it is done at once: WMS back, the ARKOD layer is drawn again; WFS back, the outlines still missing are asked; KAT or ZK back, an open sheet that could not be read is read again.

Fetch when available. When a sheet cannot be read (the state does not answer and this device has no copy), the sheet offers Fetch when available. It hands the request to the app's server: a background service on Cloudflare asks the state again every 10 minutes, as long as it takes, and keeps the answer in the private archive ARKOD_cache. Open the parcel again later: the sheet comes from the server's answer, marked "fetched later" with the time, and the same answer reaches every phone and computer that asks for it. Settings → Services → Waiting for the state lists what is still waited for, since when, how many tries, and the state's last reason.

12. The three maps and Google

  • OSM OpenStreetMap: the default, free.
  • OFF offline: Android the whole of Croatia on the phone (a 176 MB download, offered in the middle of the screen the first time; four styles in the settings). web the map tiles already seen, from the device.
  • GOO Google's map, with your own Google key (the app has none built in). The first time, the app shows the steps to make one in Google Cloud Console (a project, billing on, "Map Tiles API" and optionally "Places API (New)", a key restricted to the app). Paste the key or pick the file it is in; it is tested at once and Google's own answer is shown. Road map by default: Google refuses satellite tiles to accounts in the EEA.

The ARKOD lines are drawn over all three, in an ink that reads on each.

13. What happens underneath

  • The parcel lines are the state's WMS pictures (512 pixels a tile, from z14). The app recolours them on the device (colour, transparency, thickness) and keeps them for 60 days.
  • A tap asks the WMS which parcel is at that point (GetFeatureInfo, a fifth of a second). The outline at once is traced from the state's own picture round the finger; the exact outline comes from the WFS, which is slow and sometimes down.
  • Sheets come from OSS: the cadastre's parcel-info (posjedovni list), the land registry's lr-unit (vlasnički list), the number search.
  • The web app asks the state through its own small proxy (arkod-layer.pages.dev/api), because the state's services do not allow a web page to read them directly. Nothing is stored there.
  • What is kept, where: tiles, sheets, searches, outlines, Moje čestice, caches, Imenik, keys and the service log are on your device only (Android the app's storage; web the browser's storage for this site). Clearing the app's data, or the site's data in the browser, removes them. Export your Moje čestice as files to keep them safe.
  • The state's own reasons are shown as they give them. ORA-01000: maximum open cursors exceeded is their Oracle database running out of connections; it lasts until they restart it. Some hours they are slow or down. The app cannot fix that; it works round it by keeping everything, reading ahead while they answer, trying again, and saying what happens.

14. When something does not work

Look at the lights first. A red light is the state's service (or your signal), and Settings → Services says what cannot be done while it is red.

  • No parcel lines: zoom to z14 or closer; is ▦ lit? Is WMS red? Lines kept on the device still show.
  • A tap says nothing: zoom to z15; WMS red means the state cannot say which parcel it is. Your own and cached parcels still select.
  • A sheet does not open: KAT or ZK red. Sheets opened before open from the device, with the date they were read.
  • A search finds nothing: add the k.o. (1358/3 kukljica), or move the map over that k.o. once.
  • A parcel cache stops with ORA-01000: the app waits and retries; if it gives up, stop and start it again later. What was read is kept.
  • A service is red: press its Check now in Settings → Services; the state's database often comes back within seconds.
  • A sheet will not open: press Fetch when available on it; the server keeps asking and the sheet is there next time.
  • It worked in the morning and not in the evening: open the Service log: it shows the hours.
